## Headline cards / 标题卡片

`news/*.png` are original layouts made for this project by `scripts/build_news_wall.py`. They quote published headlines verbatim, with outlet and date, for commentary and reference; each links to the original article in the README. No publisher photos, logos or page screenshots are used. Headlines remain the property of their publishers.

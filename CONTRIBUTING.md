# Contributing / 参与贡献

Thanks for helping people prepare. / 感谢你帮助更多人做好准备。

## What we welcome / 欢迎的内容

- Tested, low-cost, practical advice (tell us what you actually tried) / 经过实践检验的低成本实用建议
- Region-specific notes (laws, climate, suppliers) / 针对特定地区的补充（法规、气候、购买渠道）
- Corrections with sources / 附带来源的纠错
- Translations — please keep `en/` and `zh/` in sync / 翻译——请保持 `en/` 与 `zh/` 同步

## What we don't accept / 不接受的内容

- Anything about attacking, sabotaging or tampering with infrastructure or people / 任何攻击、破坏基础设施或伤害他人的内容
- Weapons manufacturing instructions / 武器制造教程
- Illegal activity / 违法内容
- Product spam and affiliate links / 广告与推广链接

## Style / 风格

- English is the default; every English change should have a matching Chinese change (or open an issue asking for translation) / 默认英文；英文改动应有对应的中文改动（或开 issue 请求翻译）
- Safety warnings stay in / 安全警告不得删除
- Cite public sources for factual claims / 事实性陈述请注明公开来源

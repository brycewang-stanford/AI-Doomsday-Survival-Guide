# Chapter 8 — Medicine Without the System

[← Back to README](../README.md) | [中文](../zh/08-medical.md)

Hospitals may keep running under an AI regime — but access may depend on compliance, identity checks or rationing algorithms. Build the ability to handle the **common and treatable** yourself.

> ⚠️ This chapter is not medical advice. Take a real course. Consult real clinicians while you still can.

## 8.1 Skills beat kits

Training ranked by value:

1. **First aid + CPR** (Red Cross or equivalent) — a weekend
2. **Stop the Bleed** — a few hours; teaches tourniquets and wound packing
3. **Wilderness First Aid / Wilderness First Responder** — medicine for when help is hours or days away
4. **Community Emergency Response Team (CERT)** or local equivalent — free in many places

At least **two people** in your group should be trained, so one can treat the other.

## 8.2 Kit

**Trauma**: tourniquet (a genuine, name-brand one — counterfeits fail), pressure bandages, hemostatic gauze, gloves, trauma shears

**Wounds**: saline or clean water for irrigation, sterile dressings, tape, wound-closure strips, antiseptic

**Everyday**: pain relievers/fever reducers, antihistamines, anti-diarrhoeals, **oral rehydration salts**, burn gel, eye wash

**Tools**: thermometer, pulse oximeter, blood-pressure cuff (manual), tweezers, splints, headlamp

**Personal**: a supply of your prescriptions (ask your doctor about extra supply), spare glasses, dental kit

## 8.3 The big killers when systems fail

| Threat | Prevention |
|---|---|
| **Diarrhoeal disease** from dirty water | Safe water ([Ch. 3](03-water-food.md)), hand-washing, latrine far from water sources. Know how to make oral rehydration solution: 1 L clean water + 6 level teaspoons sugar + ½ level teaspoon salt (WHO/UNICEF home recipe) |
| **Wound infection** | Clean thoroughly, keep covered, watch for spreading redness, heat, fever |
| **Hypothermia / heatstroke** | Shelter, layers, shade, hydration |
| **CO poisoning** | Never burn fuel indoors without ventilation; battery CO alarm |
| **Chronic disease** without medication | Stockpile what you legally can; learn lifestyle management; know who in your community is vulnerable |
| **Dental infections** | Dental hygiene *now*; fix problems before a crisis |

## 8.4 Mental health

Long crises cause depression, anxiety and conflict. Build routines, shared meals, work with meaning, physical activity, and a culture where people can say they are not okay. See [Chapter 10](10-human-zoo.md).

# Chapter 3 — Water & Food

[← Back to README](../README.md) | [中文](../zh/03-water-food.md)

If an AI runs logistics, **food becomes the most powerful lever of control**. Anyone who can feed themselves, even partly, is harder to manage.

## 3.1 Water — first priority

| Need | Amount |
|---|---|
| Drinking | ~2–3 L per person per day |
| Drinking + basic hygiene | **~4 L (1 US gal) per person per day** |
| Pets | ~30 ml per kg of body weight per day |

**Store**
- Food-grade containers only; out of sunlight; label with date
- Treated municipal water keeps ~6 months; rotate or re-treat
- A full bathtub + a WaterBOB-style liner = ~300 L on short notice

**Treat** (use at least two methods)
- **Boil**: rolling boil for 1 minute (3 minutes above 2,000 m altitude)
- **Filter**: gravity filters or 0.1–0.2 µm hollow-fibre filters (removes bacteria and protozoa, *not* viruses or chemicals)
- **Chemical**: unscented household bleach — roughly **2 drops of 6% bleach per litre** (8 drops per gallon), wait 30 minutes; follow your local public-health guidance for the exact concentration
- **SODIS**: clear PET bottles in full sun for 6 hours (48 hours if cloudy) — free, slow, works for low-turbidity water

**Source**
- Rain-water harvesting from the roof (check local legality)
- A manual pump on an existing well — electric pumps die with the grid
- Map every stream, pond and spring within walking distance *now*

## 3.2 Food — three layers

### Layer 1: The deep pantry (buy)
Store what you already eat, and rotate it ("first in, first out").

| Staple | Shelf life (cool, dry, sealed) |
|---|---|
| White rice | 10+ years in mylar + oxygen absorbers |
| Dried beans, lentils | 10+ years sealed (older beans take longer to cook) |
| Rolled oats | 10+ years sealed |
| Honey, salt, sugar | Indefinite |
| Canned meat/fish | 2–5 years |
| Cooking oil | ~1–2 years (goes rancid — rotate) |
| Multivitamins | ~2 years |

Target: **2 weeks** fast, **3 months** within a year. Include a manual can opener and a way to cook without grid power (rocket stove, solar oven — **outdoors only**).

### Layer 2: Grow (produce)
- **Start now.** Gardening skill takes seasons to learn; the first harvest after a crisis is 3–4 months away.
- High-calorie, easy crops: **potatoes, sweet potatoes, squash, beans, corn, cassava** (in warm climates).
- High-nutrition, fast crops: leafy greens, radish, sprouts (ready in days on a windowsill).
- **Open-pollinated / heirloom seeds** — you can save and replant them. Hybrid (F1) seeds don't breed true.
- Small livestock: chickens (eggs) and rabbits (meat) give the best return per square metre. Check local laws.

### Layer 3: Preserve and forage (know)
- Drying, salting, fermenting (sauerkraut, kimchi), smoking, water-bath canning of high-acid foods
- Learn 10 edible wild plants in *your* region from a local expert — and the deadly look-alikes
- Fishing and legal trapping skills

## 3.3 AI-specific considerations

- **Don't depend on apps** for your garden or food storage. Paper notebooks for planting calendars and inventories.
- **Buy local, pay cash (where legal)** for bulk staples; loyalty cards and delivery apps build a precise profile of your stockpile.
- **Seed libraries and seed swaps** are the analog "cloud" for food security — join one.
- **Know your farmers.** A relationship with a local farm is worth more than a year of rice.

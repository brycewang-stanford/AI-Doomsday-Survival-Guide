**PRIORITIES — the Rule of Threes**
~3 min without air · ~3 h without shelter in extreme cold/heat · ~3 days without water · ~3 weeks without food
→ **Air → Shelter → Water → Food → Communications → Community**

**IF THE GRID GOES DARK**
1. Gather household → go to rally point if apart
2. Fill every container with water *now*
3. Analog radio on; cross-check every claim
4. Verify "official" orders in person
5. Check on vulnerable neighbours
6. Nothing about supplies or plans online

**AI-ERA RULES**
- A screen is a claim, not a fact
- Voice and video can be faked → **code word + call-back**
- Big decisions only **face to face**
- No connected devices in the shelter or at meetings
- Paper beats cloud

**SHELTER SAFETY**
- No fuel burning inside (CO kills silently) · CO alarm on
- Headache or drowsiness → **ventilate now** (CO₂)
- Two exits · openable from inside
- Never go underground in flood/surge zones
- Never work alone in an excavation over 1.5 m

**WATER** · 4 L/person/day
Boil 1 min (3 min above 2,000 m) · or 2 drops 6% bleach per L, wait 30 min · or filter + treat

**ORAL REHYDRATION** · 1 L clean water + 6 level tsp sugar + ½ level tsp salt

**EARTHQUAKE** · Drop · Cover · Hold On · coast + strong shaking → high ground

**TYPHOON / FLOOD** · Run from water, hide from wind · never walk through floodwater

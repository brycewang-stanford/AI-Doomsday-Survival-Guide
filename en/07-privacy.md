# Chapter 7 — Privacy & Low Signature

[← Back to README](../README.md) | [中文](../zh/07-privacy.md)

A superintelligent system that sees everything connected doesn't need to search for you — you report yourself, every minute, through your devices. This chapter is about **lawfully reducing how much of yourself you broadcast**.

> Honest limit: you cannot become invisible to a system like this. The goal is to be **less legible, less interesting, and less dependent** — to keep a zone of life that isn't mediated by it.

## 7.1 Your signature, ranked

| Source | What it reveals |
|---|---|
| **Smartphone** | Location every few seconds, contacts, voice, habits, sleep, relationships |
| **Payments** | What you buy, where, when — including every "prepper" purchase |
| **Smart home** | Voice assistants, cameras, doorbells, TVs, smart meters (when you're home, what you do) |
| **Car** | Connected cars log location and driving; plate readers log movement |
| **Social media** | Your beliefs, network, emotional state, weak points |
| **Faces** | Public and private cameras with facial recognition |

## 7.2 Practical steps (all legal in most places)

**Phone**
- Create **phone-free time and phone-free places** — especially your shelter and group meetings
- Turn off location history, ad ID, unused apps; review permissions
- Know how to fully power down or use a **Faraday pouch** — test that it blocks signal
- Keep a simple feature phone as a backup

**Home**
- Remove or unplug voice assistants and cloud cameras from private spaces
- Prefer devices that work fully offline
- Keep a physical key for every smart lock

**Money**
- Keep some **cash** in small denominations
- Consider tangible, tradeable goods (tools, seeds, medicine, salt, coffee, batteries) — they work when accounts are frozen
- Develop **barter relationships** and skills people will pay for

**Social**
- Don't publicly post about your preparations, supplies, shelter or group
- The "grey man" principle: look ordinary, be unremarkable
- Keep important relationships and conversations offline

## 7.3 Meeting without surveillance

- Meet outdoors or in rooms with no connected devices; leave phones behind (not just "off")
- Keep attendee lists and plans on paper, held by as few people as necessary
- Rotate places and times

## 7.4 What NOT to do

- Don't break laws. Illegal activity invites exactly the attention you want to avoid — and in a managed society, you give the system a reason.
- Don't attack, sabotage or tamper with infrastructure. It's illegal, endangers people who depend on it (hospitals, water), and is out of scope for this guide.
- Don't become paranoid to the point of isolation. Isolation is the goal of a control system; community is the answer.

# Resources

[← Back to README](../README.md) | [中文](../zh/resources.md)

Governments around the world have started telling citizens to prepare, and some now name disinformation, cyberattacks and AI explicitly. Their handbooks are free, tested and more authoritative than any prepper blog. **Download them and print them.**

## Official government handbooks

| Country / body | Document | Key recommendation | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🇸🇪 Sweden (MSB) | *If Crisis or War Comes* (updated Nov 2024, mailed to ~5 million households) | Be able to manage **on your own for a week**; ≥ **3 L water per person per day** | First edition to cover **disinformation and digital attacks**. English and many other languages |
| 🇪🇺 European Union | *Preparedness Union Strategy* (26 Mar 2025) | Every citizen should be able to manage for **at least 72 hours** | Cites hybrid threats, infrastructure sabotage, electronic warfare |
| 🇹🇼 Taiwan (All-Out Defense Mobilization Agency) | *In Case of Crisis: Taiwan's National Public Safety Guide* (Sept 2025, mailed to ~9.8 million households) | Self-sufficiency raised from **3 days to 1 week** | Warns about **deepfake videos**, fake accounts and ATMs failing in cyberattacks; family meeting-point card |
| 🇯🇵 Tokyo Metropolitan Govt | *Disaster Preparedness Tokyo* (東京防災) and *Tokyo Disaster Preparedness for Everyday Living* (updated 2023–24) | Detailed urban earthquake and flood actions | Free in **English, Simplified and Traditional Chinese**, Korean; app available |
| 🇺🇸 FEMA / Ready.gov | *Build a Kit*, *Water* | **1 gallon (~3.8 L) per person per day**; aim for **2 weeks** of self-sufficiency where possible | FEMA P-320 and P-361 have safe-room building plans |
| 🇨🇳 Ministry of Emergency Management | 全国基础版家庭应急物资储备建议清单 (Nov 2020) | 11 basic items; water ≥ **3 L per person, for 3 days** | Chinese only |
| 🇨🇳 National Disaster Reduction Committee | Household emergency supplies guidance (2024) | **Basic list (16 items)** and **extended list (31 items)** in 5 categories | Chinese only |

## Books and manuals (free or public domain)

- **Cresson Kearny, *Nuclear War Survival Skills*** (Oak Ridge National Laboratory, 1979/1987) — public domain; expedient shelters and the Kearny Air Pump
- **Hesperian, *Where There Is No Doctor* / *Where There Is No Dentist*** — free PDFs; community medicine without a hospital
- **FEMA P-320 *Taking Shelter from the Storm*** and **FEMA P-361 *Safe Rooms for Tornadoes and Hurricanes*** — engineered safe-room designs

## Research and reporting on the AI risk itself

- **Kulveit, Douglas, Ammann, Turan, Krueger & Duvenaud, "Gradual Disempowerment: Systemic Existential Risks from Incremental AI Development"** (ICML 2025 position paper). Argues that humanity could lose control not through a sudden takeover but as AI gradually replaces human roles in the **economy, culture and the state**. This is the academic version of this guide's "human zoo" ([Ch. 10](10-human-zoo.md))
- **Kokotajlo, Alexander, Lifland, Larsen & Dean, *AI 2027*** (AI Futures Project, April 2025). A month-by-month scenario forecast with two endings, one of them an AI takeover. Read it as a scenario, not a prophecy
- **Karen Hao, *Empire of AI*** (2025). Reporting from inside OpenAI, including the "bunker before AGI" remark
- **Douglas Rushkoff, *Survival of the Richest*** (2022). How the tech elite plans its escape
- **OpenAI & MIT Media Lab, *Investigating Affective Use and Emotional Well-being on ChatGPT*** (March 2025). Heavier daily use correlated with more loneliness and emotional dependence
- **FBI IC3 PSA I-120324-PSA** (Dec 2024). How criminals use generative AI, and why families should agree a secret word

## Offline tools

| Tool | What it does | Size / notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Kiwix** | Offline Wikipedia, WikiMed, Stack Exchange, Gutenberg, etc. | Full English Wikipedia: roughly **~115 GB with images, ~49 GB without, ~12 GB "mini"** (2025) |
| **Organic Maps / OsmAnd** | Offline maps and navigation from OpenStreetMap | Download your region in advance |
| **Meshtastic** | Off-grid LoRa text mesh network | Set the correct region; China uses **CN 470–510 MHz** |
| **Project Gutenberg** | 70,000+ public-domain books | Bulk download possible |

Everything digital should also exist **on paper** or on devices stored in a tested Faraday container ([Ch. 7](07-privacy.md)).

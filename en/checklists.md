# Checklists

Print these. Tick them with a pen.

## ⏱️ 72 Hours — "The grid just went dark"

- [ ] Gather household; confirm everyone's location; activate rally-point plan
- [ ] Fill every container, bathtub and pot with water while pressure lasts
- [ ] Switch to the analog radio; cross-check news with another source
- [ ] Verify any "official" instructions in person or through a trusted contact
- [ ] Do not share your supplies or plans online
- [ ] Check on elderly and disabled neighbours
- [ ] Eat perishable food first (fridge → freezer → pantry)
- [ ] Set up bucket toilet if water/sewage fails
- [ ] CO alarm on; no fuel-burning indoors

## 🗓️ 2 Weeks — Basic stockpile (per person)

- [ ] **Water**: 56 L (4 L/day) + filter + bleach/tablets
- [ ] **Food**: 14 days of no-cook or easy-cook food + manual can opener
- [ ] **Light**: LED headlamp + lantern + spare batteries
- [ ] **Power**: charged power banks + small solar charger
- [ ] **Radio**: hand-crank AM/FM/shortwave receiver
- [ ] **Comms**: walkie-talkies or LoRa mesh device; paper contact list; family code words
- [ ] **Medical**: first-aid kit, tourniquet, personal meds, ORS, spare glasses
- [ ] **Sanitation**: buckets, bags, sawdust/litter, soap, hand sanitizer, menstrual supplies
- [ ] **Warmth**: blankets, wool layers, sleeping bag
- [ ] **Money**: small-denomination cash
- [ ] **Paper**: printed guide, maps, documents copies
- [ ] **Safety**: CO alarm, CO₂ meter, fire extinguisher
- [ ] **Medical power**: battery backup (24–72 h) for any oxygen concentrator, CPAP or other powered medical device
- [ ] **Pre-commitments** written on paper: what your household will never believe or do without face-to-face confirmation ([Ch. 6](06-information.md))

## 📦 90 Days — Deepen

- [ ] 3-month rotating pantry (rice, beans, oats, oil, salt, honey, canned protein)
- [ ] Rainwater collection or access to a non-grid water source
- [ ] Offline power: 0.5–2 kWh LiFePO₄ + 100–400 W solar, cloud features disabled
- [ ] Shelter space prepared (Tier 0 or Tier 1 from [Chapter 2](02-low-cost-shelter.md))
- [ ] First aid + CPR course completed
- [ ] Group of 3–5 households formed; skills map on paper
- [ ] Neighbourhood mesh or radio net tested weekly
- [ ] Offline library: Kiwix, offline maps, printed manuals — and at least one official government handbook printed ([Resources](resources.md))
- [ ] Faraday pouch tested (cellular, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth)
- [ ] Garden started; open-pollinated seed stock

## 🌱 1 Year — Resilience

- [ ] First full harvest; seeds saved from your own plants
- [ ] Food preservation practised (drying, fermenting, canning)
- [ ] Ham radio licence (optional but valuable)
- [ ] Wilderness First Aid or equivalent
- [ ] Root cellar / earth-bermed shelter built legally (Tier 2), or prefab installed (Tier 3)
- [ ] Rural fallback location agreed with family/friends
- [ ] Group decision process and conflict resolution agreed
- [ ] Children's "analog skills" curriculum: reading, maths, maps, plants, tools
- [ ] Links with at least one other community group

## 🎒 Go-bag (per person)

- [ ] 3 L water + filter bottle
- [ ] 3 days of food
- [ ] Paper map, compass
- [ ] Headlamp, batteries
- [ ] First-aid kit, meds
- [ ] Rain layer, warm layer, hat, gloves
- [ ] Knife/multitool, duct tape, cordage
- [ ] Cash, document copies, contact list, code-word card
- [ ] Faraday pouch for phone
